# Zenith Active Solutions, Inc.
## Minutes of the Meeting of the Board of Directors

**Date:** Thursday, 12 October 2023, 14:00–16:10 ET
**Place:** Zenith Active HQ, Edison, New Jersey (conference room 2) and video link
**Directors present:** Elaine Whitford (Chair; Northgate Ventures), Marcus Lee (Independent Director), James Calloway (CEO)
**In attendance:** Priya Raman (CFO), Megan Thompson (CMO) for items 3 to 5
**Secretary:** Vivienne Okafor (General Counsel), who took these minutes

---

### 1. Opening, quorum and prior minutes

The Chair opened the meeting at 14:00 and confirmed a quorum. The minutes of the meeting of 13 July 2023 were approved without amendment.

### 2. Financial update (Q3 2023)

The CFO presented the Q3 management accounts. Q3 orders were 10,265, compared with 3,221 in Q3 2022, and gross sales were $1.25M, compared with $0.52M. The CFO reported that revenue growth had not reached the bottom line: EBITDA margin for the year to September was about 1% of net revenue, against 12% for FY2022. The largest cost increases were in marketing, discounts, warehouse labor and customer support. The CFO noted that overflow storage at StoreSafe Logistics had become a recurring cost since April.

Asked about operations, the CEO said overtime was trending up with order volume. Mr. Montgomery (Warehouse Operations Director) was not present, and the board did not discuss operations further.

### 3. Northgate Ventures: conditions for the bridge tranche

The Chair stated Northgate's position for the record. The second (bridge) tranche will be released only if the company demonstrates a **7% EBITDA margin within two quarters**, measured on the quarter ending 30 June 2024. If the plan presented in January does not credibly reach that level, Northgate will freeze the tranche. The Chair said she was "not interested in another quarter of top-line slides."

The CEO said that the team was confident and that headwinds are not stop signs. Mr. Lee asked whether 7% was negotiable. The Chair said it was not.

### 4. Marketing update and the creator program

The CMO presented the Q3 marketing results. She described the creator program, run through Pulse Creator Collective since February, as the company's main source of momentum. In Q3 there were **1,973 orders placed with creator codes**, worth **$235K in GMV**, against an agency fee of $100K for the quarter. She described this as a return of 2.3 times the fee. Creators were the largest source of new customers in the quarter. Flash-sale weekends continued to deliver the highest daily order volumes.

The Chair asked what a creator customer is worth after the first order: how many come back, and what they spend when they do. The CMO said repeat behavior in the creator cohort "looks different from other channels" and that the program was designed for reach. She did not have repeat-purchase figures available. The Chair asked for them before the January meeting.

The CFO asked whether the $235K GMV was stated before or after discounts and returns. The CMO confirmed it was gross order value as reported by the agency.

The Secretary reminded the board that the Pulse agreement runs to 31 January 2024 and renews automatically for twelve months unless written notice is given at least fourteen days before expiry, which means **by 17 January 2024**.

### 5. Proposal: "Eco-Warrior" premium line

Mr. Lee proposed that the company develop a premium eco line, provisionally called "Eco-Warrior", extending the Terra range. He argued that sustainability-focused customers are less price-sensitive and that a premium line would counter the discount-led image. The CMO supported the idea in principle. The Chair said she would not consider new product investment until the margin plan was agreed.

**Resolved:** the proposal is tabled and may be brought back once the turnaround plan has been approved.

### 6. Turnaround plan for January

The CEO asked management to present a turnaround plan at the January board meeting. The board discussed what resources might be made available. The Chair indicated that Northgate would consider a budget envelope of **up to $250K**, together with contract engineering capacity for a limited period, provided the plan showed where the margin improvement would come from. Mr. Lee asked that the plan include operations as well as marketing. The CEO agreed.

### 7. Any other business

None. The next meeting was set for **Thursday, 11 January 2024**.

The meeting closed at 16:10.
