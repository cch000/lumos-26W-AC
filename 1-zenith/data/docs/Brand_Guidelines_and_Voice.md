## 2. Voice

We sound like the friend who trains with you at 6 a.m.: warm, direct, a little bit cheeky, never smug. We are **Bold. Relentless. Real.**

- **Bold** means short sentences and strong verbs. "Lift heavier." Not "Optimise your strength journey."
- **Relentless** means we show up every day for our community. It does not mean we shout. Caps lock is for one word, once.
- **Real** means we never promise what the product can't do. No "transform your body in 7 days". No fake scarcity.

**We say:** *Built for the last rep.* · *Sweat-tested in New Jersey.* · *Your kit, your pace.*
**We don't say:** *Game-changer, revolutionary, elite, hack, beast mode, insane deal.*

Write to one person, not to "you guys". Read it out loud before you hit publish. If it sounds like a billboard, rewrite it.

## 4. Creators

Creators are how a new customer meets Zenith for the first time, so they are the most important "voice" we have that isn't ours.

**We partner with fitness creators – at all costs.** The right creator is worth more than any ad slot we could buy. Since February we work with a roster of twelve through Pulse Creator Collective, and I want every one of them to feel like part of the team: early samples, first look at drops, a say in colourways.

Rules for creator content:
1. Creators speak in their own voice. We brief, we never script.
2. Product must be shown in use, not unboxed and abandoned.
3. Every creator has a personal code. Codes are **30% off** and are the creator's gift to their community.
4. Creator posts link to the creator landing page, never straight to checkout.

## 5. Price and promotion

Zenith is a premium brand at a fair price. Our customers should feel smart buying from us, never lucky.

**We never discount deeper than 30%. Deep discounts cheapen the brand.**

- One offer per message. A customer should never have to do maths to work out what they pay.
- Sitewide moments are rare and planned. From 3 March we run one flash weekend a month: 30% off with ZENITH30, first weekend of the month, Friday to Sunday. That's the event. It should feel like one.
- Welcome and loyalty codes (WELCOME10, NEWS5, LOYAL15) live in e-mail and the account area, never on the homepage.
- We don't use "SALE" in red. We use Ember, and we say the number.
- Never put a discount on Terra in the hero image. Terra is not a clearance line.

## 6. Terra: the future of Zenith

Terra is our eco line: organic cotton, hemp, recycled fibres, cork, bamboo. Today it's a handful of products. In three years I want it to be the first thing people think of when they hear our name.

- **Look:** Terra Sage and Kraft only. Matte, earthy, textured. No Ember on Terra.
- **Words:** honest and specific. "Hemp grown without irrigation" beats "planet-friendly". Never "green", never "guilt-free".
- **Unboxing:** every Terra order is hand-wrapped in compostable wrap and tied with natural twine, with a seed-paper thank-you card. It takes longer. It's worth it. This is the moment a customer decides whether we meant it.
- **Hero products for spring:** Terra Organic Cotton Tee, Terra Hemp Shorts, Terra Cork Yoga Mat. The Eco Hydration Bottle sits with Terra in every lookbook, even though the name predates the line.

Terra customers are our most loyal fans in the making. Treat every Terra touchpoint like a first date.
