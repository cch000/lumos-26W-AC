# MEMORANDUM

**From:** Priya Raman, CFO
**To:** Board of Directors (Elaine Whitford, Marcus Lee, James Calloway); Leadership team
**Date:** 4 January 2024
**Subject:** FY2023 preliminary results and decisions needed on 11 January

---

The full board pack follows on Monday. I'm sending the headline numbers now so nobody sees them for the first time in the room.

## 1. Summary

We more than doubled the business in 2023 and earned less money doing it. Net revenue grew 2.2 times and order volume tripled, but EBITDA fell from $204K to $69K. On the preliminary numbers that is a margin of **1.9%**, against 12.0% in 2022.

Northgate's condition for the bridge tranche is a 7% EBITDA margin within two quarters. At our FY2023 revenue, 7% would have been about $257K of EBITDA. We are roughly **$188K** short of that on the preliminary figures, and the gap will widen once December closes (see section 3).

The lines that grew faster than revenue:

- **Discounts**: from 5.7% of gross sales to 14.6%.
- **Warehouse labor**: up 3.6 times. Overtime alone cost about $235K, against $24K in 2022, and that was after we added four warehouse staff in March.
- **Reshipments**: ten times the 2022 figure.
- **Customer support**: up almost six times on three times the orders.
- **Marketing**: up 2.4 times. The Pulse agency fee was $379K of the $631K (the January pilot plus eleven monthly payments).
- **Overflow storage**: a new cost line since April.

I am not attributing causes here. Each line has an owner with an explanation, and the explanations don't all agree.

## 3. December is preliminary

December is not closed. Customers can return within 30 days, and creator-code orders get 60, so a large share of December's returns has not arrived yet. December's booked return rate is 4.8%, against 7.1% in November. I don't believe the drop. I expect December to end up close to November once late returns are processed.

Refunds for December will therefore rise, and FY2023 EBITDA will come down with them. My working estimate is that the final FY2023 margin lands **closer to 1.5%** than 1.9%. Please don't read December's apparently strong month ($29K EBITDA on the preliminary books) as a recovery.

## 4. Decisions requested on 11 January

1. **Turnaround plan.** Approve one plan aimed at the 7% target, with a budget of up to **$250K** and **two contract engineers for one quarter**. I'd ask that any plan put to the board shows where the margin comes from line by line, and over what timeframe.
2. **Pulse Creator Collective.** The agreement ($400K a year) renews automatically for twelve months on 1 February unless we give written notice by **17 January**. That is six days after the board meeting, so we need a decision in the room: renew, renegotiate, or give notice.
