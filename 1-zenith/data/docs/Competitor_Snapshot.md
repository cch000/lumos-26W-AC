Everything comes from their websites, checkout flows (I stopped at the payment page), help centers, two podcast interviews and LinkedIn. Some of it is my interpretation.

## Ridgeline Athletics

**What they are:** DTC apparel and supplements, similar size to us or a bit bigger, based in Colorado. Their range is narrower than ours. I counted around 80 products on the site.

**What stood out:**
- **Supplements subscription is front and center.** Every protein, creatine and greens product page defaults to "Subscribe & Save 10%", with auto-ship every 4, 5 or 6 weeks. You can skip or pause from your account page. One-time purchase is the second option, in smaller text.
- **They hardly ever discount.** I went back through their Instagram and e-mail archive (I signed up in September). I found one sitewide sale in the last six months, a short Labor Day weekend offer. Otherwise they run "member early access" to new colorways instead of price promos.
- **Repeat is clearly their model.** In a podcast interview their founder said most of their revenue comes from returning customers and that subscriptions are "the floor of the business". She didn't give a number.
- Returns: 30 days, free exchanges, refunds minus a $5 return label.

**Interesting for us?** Supplements buyers seem to behave differently from apparel buyers. I'm not sure we know what our supplements customers do after their first order.

## Kinetic Supply Co.

**What they are:** Equipment-led brand (kettlebells, bands, mats) that has added apparel. West Coast based. They're very active with creators, possibly more than us.

**What stood out:**
- **Their creator program is paid on retained customers.** From their creator-recruitment page and two creator posts about it: creators get a small flat fee, plus a bonus for each customer who places a second order within 90 days. One creator described it as "you get paid when they come back, not when they click."
- **Creator codes don't combine with anything.** I tried a creator code plus their holiday promo in checkout and got: "Only one offer can be applied per order." The code gives 15% off.
- **Standard 30-day returns** on everything, creator orders included.
- They work with many smaller creators rather than a few big ones (30+ tagged in the last two months).

**Interesting for us?** It's a different way of thinking about what a creator is paid for. I'd guess the big creators don't love it.
