# Product reviews export: zenithactive.com

**Source:** ReviewPad storefront app, "Export reviews" (CSV, converted for the board data room)
**Period:** 1 Jul 2023 – 14 Dec 2023
**Selection:** 16 reviews, a random sample plus reviews flagged by CX for follow-up (flag shown)
**Exported:** 15 Dec 2023 by the marketing team. Reviewer names shortened by the app; text unedited.

---

**★★★★★ | Rachel D. | 9 Jul 2023 | Whey Isolate Vanilla 2lb Bundle 2x | Verified buyer**
I've been ordering this every month since spring. Mixes clean in a shaker, no clumps, and the vanilla isn't that fake cake-frosting taste. The bundle lasts me almost exactly four weeks, so I just reorder when the second bag is half empty. Wish there was an auto-ship option so I don't have to remember.

**★★☆☆☆ | Brittany K. | 21 Jul 2023 | Core Seamless Leggings - Navy | Verified buyer | CX flag: return**
Honestly I only bought these because FITFAM did a whole video on them and her code made it 30% off. They're fine I guess, but way more see-through in the squat test than in her video. Sent them back. The 60-day return window was nice at least. Wouldn't buy at full price.

**★★★★★ | Darnell T. | 2 Aug 2023 | Hex Dumbbell Pair 10kg Bundle 2x | Verified buyer**
Solid, no wobble, the hex shape means they don't roll around my garage floor. Shipped in a heavy box that survived the trip. My second set.

**★★★☆☆ | Kevin L. | 16 Aug 2023 | Performance Tank - Black | Verified buyer**
Tank is good, breathes well, true to size. Three stars because of the website. I wanted the tank AND the matching shorts, but as soon as I added the tank the site dumped me into checkout. Couldn't figure out how to get back, so I paid for the tank and figured I'd order the shorts later. Haven't yet.

**★★★★★ | Sophie W. | 31 Aug 2023 | Eco Hydration Bottle - Sand | Verified buyer**
Ordered on a Tuesday and it was on my porch in Los Angeles on Wednesday. Keeps water cold through a full hot yoga session. The sand colour is lovely.

**★★☆☆☆ | Daniel R. | 6 Sep 2023 | Velocity Socks 3-Pack - Black | Verified buyer | CX flag: wrong item**
Ordered black, received olive. The packing slip literally says Black. Support was friendly and sent the right ones, but it took another week, and I had to print a return label for a $22 pack of socks.

**★★★☆☆ | Hannah P. | 18 Sep 2023 | Alpine Zip Hoodie - Charcoal | Verified buyer | CX flag: shipping**
Love the hoodie. Soft, heavy, nice zip. But after ordering I heard nothing for a week. No shipping email, no tracking, nothing. I was about to dispute the charge when it showed up. I had to message support just to find out it was on its way. Other stores send you a tracking link automatically.

**★★★★★ | Ana G. | 27 Sep 2023 | Terra Organic Cotton Tee - Burgundy | Verified buyer**
Super soft and I love that it's organic cotton. It came wrapped in compostable paper tied with twine, which felt really special. Took a few days longer than my other Zenith orders but worth it.

**★★☆☆☆ | Jess M. | 5 Oct 2023 | Terra Recycled Leggings - Red | Verified buyer**
Feels like old stock. The plastic sleeve had gone cloudy and the waistband had a hard crease, like they'd been folded in a box for a year. The fabric itself is OK. For $115 I expect them to look new.

**★★★★☆ | Owen N. | 12 Oct 2023 | Adjustable Kettlebell 16kg | Verified buyer**
Great kettlebell, the weight change is quick and the handle is comfy. One star off for the box: three thick strips of tape on every seam, and a paper insert I didn't need. Took a knife and five minutes to get in.

**★★★☆☆ | Chris B. | 24 Oct 2023 | Stride Jogger - Sand | Verified buyer**
Nice joggers, good pockets. But I've learned never to buy from Zenith unless it's 30% off. There's a sale with a countdown every month and a creator code all the time, so why would anyone pay full price? I set a calendar reminder for the first Friday. Feels a bit cheap for the brand, to be honest.

**★★☆☆☆ | Carla S. | 21 Nov 2023 | Summit Running Jacket - Navy | Verified buyer | CX flag: refund**
Didn't fit across the shoulders, so I returned it right away. The warehouse got it on the 6th (tracking says so), and the refund didn't show up until the 17th. Two emails to support. The jacket was nice, the process wasn't.

**★★★★★ | Nisha V. | 14 Nov 2023 | Creatine Monohydrate 300g | Verified buyer**
Third tub. Unflavoured and dissolves fine in coffee. Reasonably priced even without a code. Order arrives about every three weeks like clockwork, because I reorder like clockwork.

**★★★★☆ | Dev F. | 20 Nov 2023 | Velocity Training Shorts - Black | Verified buyer**
Used a creator code AND the flash sale code and got a ridiculous price, so I'm not complaining. Liner is comfortable, pockets hold a phone. Lost a star because the black faded a bit after a few washes.

**★☆☆☆☆ | Lauren H. | 1 Dec 2023 | Pulse Crop Top - Burgundy | Verified buyer | CX flag: return**
Bought with an influencer code during the sale. The colour was nothing like the photos, more brown than burgundy, and there was a loose thread at the hem. Returned. Not ordering again.

**★★★★☆ | Sam O. | 11 Dec 2023 | Terra Hemp Shorts - Red | Verified buyer**
Comfortable, a bit stiff at first but they soften up. Pretty twine wrapping. Delivery took over a week, though; my last order (socks and a shaker, nothing from Terra) came in three days.
