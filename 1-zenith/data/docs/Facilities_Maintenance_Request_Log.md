### Notes on open / high items

**WO-23-114: Green Station lighting.** The fixture over the eco packing bench started failing on 9 Aug: half the strip dead, the rest flickering. The electrician says it's too old to just swap the ballast. Ordered a matching LED fixture 11 Aug so it fits the existing ceiling mount. The supplier's date has moved twice: first end of August, then late September, now "mid-December". Interim since 10 Aug: work lights borrowed from the dock, a clamp lamp on the shelf, plus headlamps. The team says it's workable during the day shift. Evenings are hard, especially reading pick lists and checking colours. An in-stock fixture exists but needs a new mount and an electrical permit. Asked Harold on 14 Nov whether to go ahead; waiting on his OK.

**WO-23-126: Aisle 7 near-miss.** A picker coming out of aisle 7 with a loaded cart met a pallet jack in the cross-aisle on the way to the Green Station. No contact, no injury. The corner is blind because of the end-cap shelving. Convex mirror installed 6 Oct. R. Gupta says traffic on that cross-aisle is "constant" now, since every eco order goes that way. Suggested a one-way loop; Harold to decide.

**WO-23-130: Green Station staging.** Totes waiting for eco packing are stacked three high on the floor next to the bench, sometimes into the cross-aisle. Trip hazard. A folding table is there as a stopgap. A proper staging rack would need to come out of the 2024 budget.

**WO-23-139: Dock door 2.** Second time this year. The spring was replaced in August; the track now looks bent, maybe from a forklift. Vendor coming 5 Dec.
