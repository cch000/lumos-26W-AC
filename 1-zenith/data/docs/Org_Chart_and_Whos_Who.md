**Megan Thompson, CMO.** Runs brand, performance marketing, the creator program and flash weekends.

**Jordan Blake, CX Lead.** Help desk, returns approvals and the CX team rota.

**Brendan Ohs, IT Manager.** Our whole IT department. Leading the Slack to Microsoft Teams migration (cutover 2 January).

**Vivienne Okafor, General Counsel.** Part-time (Tuesdays and Thursdays). Contracts, compliance, board minutes.

**Alex Thompson, freelance developer (former).** Built the in-house services for the BF22 relaunch (November 2022). His contract ended 28 February 2023. His README is on the server. No relation to Megan.
