## Part B: Zenith packing instructions

**6.1 Every order, every station**

1. Check the items against the pick list. Initial the list.
2. Use the smallest box that fits without forcing.
3. Paper void fill to stop movement. No plastic air pillows.
4. Place the brand card on top of the items, logo up, so it is the first thing the customer sees. Place the returns slip underneath it.
5. Seal bottom and top with **three strips** of paper tape each: one along the centre seam and one across each end, overlapping the sides by at least 2 in. Every box, including the mailer. We had crushed corners in 2021; the three-strip rule fixed it.
6. Packing slip in the clear pouch on the side, label on top.

**6.2 Orders with more than 3 units**

Packed only after the pick list carries the supervisor stamp (see SOP 4.1, step 7). Unstamped lists go back to the stamp desk.

**6.3 Eco (Terra) orders**

Any order that contains a Terra item or an Eco Hydration Bottle is packed **at the Green Station only**. The Green Station is the one place stocked with the compostable wrap and the jute twine.

1. Wrap each eco item in one sheet of compostable wrap, folded, not taped.
2. Tie with twine, a single bow on top. Tuck a sprig card ("Packed with care. Terra by Zenith") under the knot.
3. Non-eco items in the same order go into the box unwrapped, under the eco items.
4. Box and seal as in 6.1 (three strips, brand card, returns slip).
5. Never wrap eco items at the main benches. The Green Station look must be consistent, and wrap and twine are counted only there.

**6.4 Damaged boxes**

Any box with a crushed corner or a tear goes in the recycling cage. Never patch a box with tape.
