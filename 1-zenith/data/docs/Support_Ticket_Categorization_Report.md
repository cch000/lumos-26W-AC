## What I see

**WISMO is now half of everything we do.** "Where is my order?" was 48% of tickets this year (3,743 of 7,724), against about 29% of a much smaller pile in 2022. It didn't just grow with orders, it grew *per order*: through 2022 and January 2023 we ran at roughly 3 WISMO tickets per 100 orders. It started climbing in the second half of February, hit 12 per 100 in March and has sat at 11–13 every month since. No spring recovery, and no spike around flash weekends either.

What the agents hear, word for word, over and over: "I never got a shipping confirmation", "is my order even coming?", "I didn't get a tracking link." In almost every case the order has shipped and the tracking number is right there in Shopify; we paste it and the customer is happy. A two-minute ticket, but at almost 500 a month it adds up, and the tone is getting sharper. Several regulars say they "used to get an e-mail". My working assumption is that slower delivery makes people anxious earlier. I'd welcome another explanation.

**Wrong-item tickets have more than doubled per order.** We averaged about 1 wrong-item ticket per 100 orders in 2022. In November it was 5. Usually a different colour of the same product (Black socks instead of Sand, Burgundy leggings instead of Navy), sometimes a missing second item. Each one means a reship plus a return label.

**Returns tickets are odd in one specific way.** Volume roughly tracks orders. But agents keep flagging creator-code returns that the system approves long after the 60-day window: 70, 90, sometimes more than 100 days after the order. The returns screen shows them as eligible, so agents process them. By my team's own count it was roughly 25–30 in October and November alone. Standard (non-creator) orders past 30 days get rejected as they should. I've passed examples to Vivienne.

**Other** is mostly address changes, Klarna questions and discount codes, including people surprised that a creator code plus the flash-sale code came out bigger than expected. Agents honour whatever checkout charged.

## The ask

We are four agents. At December volume, chats wait 6–8 hours on weekdays and over a day after flash weekends. I'm asking for **two additional agents from January** (temp-to-perm is fine), plus cover on flash weekends.

Separately, and cheaper: a self-serve "track my order" page on the site would answer a good share of WISMO by itself. I'd also like someone to look at why the returns tool lets creator orders through after 60 days.
