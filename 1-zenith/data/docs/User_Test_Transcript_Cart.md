# User tests: add-to-cart flow (round 1)

**Researcher:** Nadia Kowalski
**Sent to:** Megan Thompson, Jamie Sinclair, Jordan Blake; cc Brendan Ohs
**Date of notes:** 12 December 2023
**Sessions:** 3 remote moderated sessions, 30 minutes each, on the live site. Fri 1 Dec (P1, P2) and Sat 2 Dec (P3), so the December flash sale was running.

**Task given to every participant:** "You want a new outfit for the gym. Buy a Performance Tank and a pair of Velocity Training Shorts, any colour and size. Go as far as the payment page, then stop."

---

## Participants

| | Device | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | iPhone | Man, 34, Chicago. Runs three times a week. Bought from Zenith once, last spring. |
| P2 | Android | Woman, 27, Austin. Never bought from Zenith. Follows two fitness creators on social media. |
| P3 | Desktop (Windows, Chrome) | Man, 45, central New Jersey. Buys protein online every month, mostly from a competitor. |

---

## P1 (iPhone)

Found the Performance Tank via MEN, picked Black, size M, tapped ADD TO CART.

> "OK, added, nice. — Wait. Where am I?"

The modal appeared and the page moved to checkout before he did anything. He scrolled the checkout page for a few seconds looking for a link back.

> "There's no 'keep shopping'. I'll just go back." *(uses the browser back button)*

Back on the product page, he searched "shorts", found Velocity Training Shorts, picked Black, added them, and was sent to checkout again. This time both items were in the order.

> "Fine, it worked, but it felt like the site was rushing me. Like a salesperson walking you to the till while you're still holding the first shirt."

On the hero: "Ends midnight… 7 hours. OK, so I should decide today?" He didn't comment on it again.

**Outcome:** both items in the order, after one detour.

## P2 (Android)

Arrived on the homepage and immediately read out the trust strip: "Oh, FITFAM30, I know her!" She went to WOMEN, found Velocity Training Shorts in Olive and added them. The modal flashed and she was in checkout.

> "Huh. Did I buy them? No, OK, it's just checkout."

She looked for a way back, tapped the order summary, then the logo, which reloaded checkout.

> "I don't know how to get out of here. I'll just get the shorts and do the tank another time."

She entered ZENITH30 and FITFAM30 in the two code fields ("the banner said stack it, so I'm stacking it") and seemed pleased with the total. Then she noticed a shipping charge had appeared.

> "Oh, and now there's shipping. Annoying. Where did it say that?"

Asked what she thought of the homepage: "Very pink. Very sale. Like, is anything ever full price here?"

**Outcome:** shorts only. Didn't go back for the tank.

## P3 (desktop)

The most deliberate of the three. He read the hero aloud and laughed.

> "'Limited time only.' I was on this site at the start of November and it was thirty percent off, 'ends midnight', then too. And in October, I think. So it never really ends. That's not a sale, that's the price."

He chose the Performance Tank in Sand and clicked ADD TO CART. He saw the modal, moved his mouse towards "Continue Shopping" and missed it: the page changed while the cursor was on its way.

> "I was going for that. It didn't let me."

At checkout he sat back. "I guess they want me to buy one thing at a time." Prompted that he still needed the shorts, he said he would "probably come back for them later, or not."

Later, unprompted: "If I bought protein here I'd add a shaker. Not if it throws me out every time."

**Outcome:** tank only. Didn't attempt the shorts.

---

## Patterns

1. **All three were moved to checkout after the first add-to-cart.** None of them chose to go there.
2. **Two of three gave up on the second item.** One went back with the browser button and re-added. Nobody used "Continue Shopping"; one tried and missed.
3. **Nobody found an in-page way back from checkout.** The logo does not go to the catalogue.
4. **The discount messaging registers, but not as a positive.** All three mentioned the 30% banners or the countdown without being asked. P3 openly distrusts an "always-on" sale. P2 combined two codes because the banner told her to.
5. **Shipping came as a surprise.** P2 only found out at checkout; the cart never mentions the free-shipping threshold.

## Recommendations (repeat from the September audit, now with evidence)

- Remove the automatic jump to checkout. Keep a confirmation, with two equal buttons.
- Add a persistent mini-cart with "goes well with" suggestions and a free-shipping progress line.
- Make the logo on checkout return to the shop.
- Rethink the daily countdown.

Three people is a small sample, but the pattern was the same in every session. Happy to run a second round once a fix is on staging.
