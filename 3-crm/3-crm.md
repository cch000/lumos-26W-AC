# Customer Relationship Management

*Disclaimer: This is a real problem at Lumos. We do not go into detail on how we solve it internally, in order to see how AC participants approach it from scratch. Our current solution is functional, but contains too much friction, especially for non-technical users.*

### Problem

A core mission of Lumos is to connect students with companies by entering into partnerships of many kinds: paid workshops, booking venues, marketing posts... you name it. To accomplish this, Lumos needs to stay in touch with partners via email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or even in person. The problem is that keeping track of those interactions is difficult, which is why the organization needs a customer relationship management (CRM) system.

- Over time, it becomes difficult to keep track of responsibilities and deliverables on both sides of a partnership agreement.
- Outreach scales poorly without a solid system in place.
- Undocumented knowledge is lost over time (like when an individuals leave the organization).

Lumos needs a CRM to keep track of partner interactions in a way that balances effectiveness against the friction of maintaining it. Anonymized samples based on real Lumos interaction notes are provided in the folder `/track-1/data/`.
