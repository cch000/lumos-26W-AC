# Brainstorm: "Eco-Warrior" premium line
**When:** Mon 30 Oct 2023, 14:00–15:30, Edison, creative studio (Marcus dialled in for the first half)
**Who:** Marcus Lee (board), Jamie Sinclair (Creative), Sofia P. (Growth, notes)
**Status:** raw ideas, not a plan. Nothing here is approved.

---

### Where this came from
Marcus raised it at the October board: a premium eco line that goes further than Terra. Board tabled it, Marcus asked Jamie to "dream big and bring it back". This was that session.

### The big idea
**Eco-Warrior**: performance kit made from recovered ocean plastic and plant-based foams. Premium price, limited drops, a mission you can wear.
- Marcus: "Terra says we care. Eco-Warrior says we fight."
- Jamie: Terra stays the everyday eco line. Eco-Warrior sits above it. Darker palette (deep sea blue, kelp green), still no Ember.
- Positioning line we liked best: **"Train like the planet depends on it."** Runner-up: "Every rep, a rescue."

### Product ideas (concepts only, nothing sampled)
- Ocean-yarn leggings + matching sports bra (hero)
- Kelp-foam yoga mat, thicker than the cork mat
- Recovered-net training shorts
- Refill-station water bottle with a "bottles saved" counter on the cap
- Marcus would also like a supplement: "algae protein, call it Riptide". Jamie not convinced it fits.
- Price idea: roughly 25–30% above Terra. "People pay for meaning."

### Why now (from the room)
- Jamie: Terra already proves our customers want this. The Terra Recycled Leggings and the Cork Yoga Mat Bundle are the most beautiful things we make, and creators love shooting them.
- Marcus: Gen Z buys values. Competitors are all doing "eco" as a colourway; nobody owns it as a mission yet.
- Both: it gives the brand a story beyond the 30% weekends.

### Launch concept
- **Earth Day 2024 (22 April)** launch. Teaser from March.
- Creator-led: brief Pulse to find two or three ocean-conservation creators. Marcus: "the best ones won't be cheap and that's fine."
- Beach clean-up weekend at Sandy Hook with creators + customers, filmed for a mini-doc.
- Packaging: hand-wrapped like Terra, plus a seed-paper insert and a tag telling you which beach the plastic came from.
- 1% of Eco-Warrior sales to an ocean charity (Sofia to shortlist).

### Budget
Nobody has costed this. Jamie's gut feel for a first production run plus the launch campaign and the mini-doc: somewhere around $150–200K. Marcus: "If we're doing it, we do it properly. Half-hearted eco is worse than none."
