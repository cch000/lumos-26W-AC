# CREATOR AGENCY SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:** Zenith Active Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices in Edison, New Jersey ("**Client**")
**And:** Pulse Creator Collective LLC, a California limited liability company with offices in Los Angeles, California ("**Agency**")

**Effective Date:** 15 December 2022
**Client business owner:** Megan Thompson, Chief Marketing Officer
**Agreement No.:** PCC-2022-117

*Excerpt prepared for internal reference. Sections 9 to 13 (intellectual property, confidentiality, indemnification, liability, general) are omitted. The executed copy is held by Legal.*

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Creator**" means an individual content creator represented by Agency and listed in Schedule B, as updated from time to time by mutual written agreement.

1.2 "**Creator Code**" means a unique promotional code assigned to a Creator under Schedule B, which customers may enter at Client's online checkout.

1.3 "**Creator Order**" means any order placed on Client's online store on which a valid Creator Code has been applied.

1.4 "**GMV**" means the gross merchandise value of Creator Orders at list price, before discounts, returns, refunds, taxes and shipping charges.

1.5 "**Campaign Content**" means posts, stories, short-form video, livestreams and other content produced by Creators promoting Client's products.

## 2. Services

2.1 Agency shall recruit, contract and manage the Creators and coordinate the production and publication of Campaign Content on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube in accordance with a quarterly content calendar agreed with Client's Chief Marketing Officer.

2.2 Agency shall ensure that each Creator publishes no fewer than four (4) pieces of Campaign Content per calendar month, at least one of which shall include a direct call to action featuring the Creator's Creator Code.

2.3 Agency shall be responsible for all payments to Creators. Client shall have no direct payment obligation to any Creator.

2.4 Agency shall ensure that all Campaign Content complies with applicable advertising disclosure requirements, including clear disclosure of the commercial relationship.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 **Pilot.** Agency shall run a pilot during January 2023 (the "**Pilot Period**") on the terms set out in Schedule A.

3.2 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement begins on **1 February 2023** and ends on **31 January 2024** (the "**Initial Term**").

3.3 **Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**") on the same terms, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal **no later than fourteen (14) days before the expiry** of the then-current term.

## 4. Fees

4.1 Client shall pay Agency the fees set out in Schedule A.

4.2 Fees are fixed and are not contingent on sales, GMV or any other performance measure, except as expressly stated in Schedule A.

4.3 Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 **Category exclusivity.** During the Term, Client shall not engage any fitness, training or athletic-lifestyle content creator for paid promotion of Client's products except through Agency.

5.2 Client shall terminate its existing direct arrangements with creators by 31 January 2023. Agency shall offer representation to those creators, and any creator who accepts shall be listed in Schedule B.

5.3 Agency shall ensure that no Creator promotes a directly competing fitness apparel or equipment brand during the Term.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 Agency shall deliver a monthly performance report by the tenth (10th) business day of the following month, containing for each Creator: (a) impressions; (b) engagement; (c) number of Creator Orders; and (d) GMV.

6.2 Agency shall deliver a quarterly business review summarizing the above measures.

6.3 For the purposes of this Agreement, the agreed performance measures are **GMV and impressions**. Client shall provide Agency with order-level data for Creator Orders (order date, Creator Code and order value) on a weekly basis to enable reporting.

## 7. Client Obligations

7.1 Client shall honor all Creator Codes on the terms in Schedule B.

7.2 Client shall configure its online store and checkout so that Creator Codes are applied in accordance with Schedule B.

7.3 Client shall provide product samples to Creators at no charge, up to a value agreed in each quarterly content calendar.

## 8. Termination

8.1 **For cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within that period.

8.2 **For convenience.** Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying a termination fee equal to **twenty-five percent (25%)** of the Fees that would have been payable for the remainder of the then-current term.

8.3 Upon termination or expiry, all Creator Codes shall be deactivated within five (5) business days. Client's obligations under Schedule B, Section 2.5 survive for Creator Orders placed before deactivation.

### 2. Creator Code Terms

2.1 **Discount.** Each Creator Code gives the customer **thirty percent (30%)** off the list price of eligible products.

2.2 **Eligibility.** Creator Codes apply sitewide, excluding gift cards. Creator Codes may be used by new and existing customers, with no limit on the number of uses per customer.

2.3 **Non-combination.** Creator Codes **are not combinable with any other promotion, discount or code**, including sitewide sales, newsletter or loyalty codes and other Creator Codes. Where a customer attempts to combine a Creator Code with another discount, only one discount shall apply.

2.4 **Attribution.** A Creator Order is attributed to the Creator whose Creator Code was applied, regardless of the customer's prior purchase history.

2.5 **Returns.** Client shall offer a return window of **sixty (60) days** from the order date on all Creator Orders, and shall state this in Creator Campaign Content and at checkout. Returned items must be unworn or unused and in original condition.

2.6 **Promotional calendar.** Client shall give Agency at least ten (10) days' notice of any sitewide sale so that Creators may plan Campaign Content around it.
