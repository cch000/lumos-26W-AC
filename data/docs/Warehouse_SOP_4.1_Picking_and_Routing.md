## SOP 4.1: Order Picking and Routing

### 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how every customer order is picked, routed and handed to packing, so that each order leaves this building complete and correct and any mistake can be traced to one list, one tote and one set of initials. Follow it as written. If a step cannot be followed, call the shift supervisor. Do not improvise.

### 2. Principles

**One order, one trip.** A picker works one pick list at a time, from the first line to the last, and brings that order back before starting another. Orders are never mixed in a tote, so there is no sorting step, which is where most mix-ups happen.

**Paper you can sign.** The printed pick list travels with the tote from printer to packing bench and is filed after shipping.

**Two sets of eyes on large orders.** Large orders carry the most value and the most room for error, so a supervisor checks them before packing.

### 3. Equipment

- Pick-list printer at the packing area (lists print oldest order first)
- One grey tote per order; green tote for orders containing eco items
- Blue pen; supervisor stamp ("VERIFIED", with date and initials) at the stamp desk

### 4. Procedure

1. **Take the next list.** Take the top list from the printer tray. Do not choose lists.
2. **Check for eco items.** Lines marked with a green **T** are Terra or Eco Hydration Bottle items. If the list has any, take a green tote; otherwise a grey tote.
3. **Walk the standard loop.** Start at the packing area and follow the marked route: up aisle 1, down aisle 2, and so on through aisle 9, returning along the main cross-aisle. The full loop averages about **300 ft**. Pick lines in the order they appear on the list; the list is printed in loop order. Do not take shortcuts between racks.
4. **Pick and tick.** At each bin, confirm the SKU number, product name and colour against the list. Tick the line in blue pen only once the item is in the tote. Never pick by colour alone: Velocity Socks, Core Seamless Leggings and Terra apparel sit in neighbouring bins by colour.
5. **Short picks.** If a bin is empty or short, write "SP" next to the line and complete the rest. Do not substitute another colour or size. Give short-picked lists to the shift supervisor.
6. **Route eco orders to the Green Station.** A green tote does not go to the main packing benches. At the end of the loop, take it to the Green Station along the east wall and place it on the incoming shelf. This adds roughly **400 ft** to the trip. Eco items are wrapped and packed only at the Green Station (see Packing Instructions, section 6.3).
7. **Supervisor stamp for large orders.** Any order with **more than 3 units** in total goes to the stamp desk before packing. The shift supervisor checks every unit against the list and stamps the list "VERIFIED". The packer does not open a box for an unstamped list. If the supervisor is away, the tote waits on the stamp shelf.
8. **Hand over.** Place the tote and list at the next free packing bench (or the Green Station shelf), initial the list, and return to the printer for the next one.

### 5. Responsibilities and training

Pickers own steps 1–6 and 8. The shift supervisor owns step 7, short picks, and keeping the stamp desk staffed. Packers refuse any list that is unticked, uninitialled or, over 3 units, unstamped. New pickers walk the loop with a supervisor for two full shifts before picking alone.
