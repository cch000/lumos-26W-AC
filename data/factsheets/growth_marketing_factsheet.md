## Useful unit economics (finance, FY2023)
- Product cost (COGS) averages about 32% of gross sales.
- Shipping averages $9 per order (Zenith pays the carrier on every order); payment fees ~3%.
- A returned order is refunded in full; about 60% of the returned goods' cost is written off and the return label costs $6.50.
- Agency fee: $400,000/yr ($33,333/month) since Feb 2023; pilot $12,000 in Jan 2023.
